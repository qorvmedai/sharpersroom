Guest Thought Leader

Dr. Tinu Smith

CEO, Datina Designs Fashion House & School | Founder, She Grows Africa & Datcore Business Coaching Academy

30+ Years Experience Cornell Alumna Goldman Sachs Scholar UN Fashion Impact Fund 2022 Oyo State Woman of the Year

Dr. Tinu Smith is an award-winning Nigerian fashion entrepreneur, business coach, and thought leader with over 30 years of experience building and scaling sustainable ventures. She is the Chief Executive Officer of Datina Designs Fashion House and Fashion School, a leading Afrocentric brand with retail presence in Ibadan and Lagos.

Through DatinaDesigns, she delivers Afrocentric ready-to-wear collections to a growing international clientele, with distribution across Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, and the United Kingdom, positioning African fashion within the global market.

Dr. Tinu holds a background in Communication and Language Arts and is an alumna of Cornell University, where she studied Women's Entrepreneurship. She is also a Goldman Sachs Scholar from the Enterprise Development Centre at Pan-Atlantic University and holds an MBA from the International School of Management, Lagos.

She has collaborated on high-impact initiatives with organizations such as the Mastercard Foundation, Industrial Training Fund, and the United Nations Fashion Impact Fund. Her work focuses on women and youth empowerment, financial inclusion, and building scalable business systems.

As the Founder of She Grows Africa and Datcore Business Coaching Academy, Dr. Tinu has empowered over 10,000 women through vocational and entrepreneurial training. She mentors for global platforms including Tony Elumelu Foundation, Cherie Blair Foundation for Women, and WIMBIZ.

"A Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Corporate Mentoring and Coaching Nigeria, Dr. Tinu delivers actionable insights on entrepreneurship, business growth, women's empowerment, and building globally relevant African brands."
